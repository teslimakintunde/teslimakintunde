       <img src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/650ad291fd6cf342753e6a79/6ad4d1cabbd6c56e8940e05f_Bank%20Customer%20Churn%20%26%20Retention%20Infographic.png" width="600" height="400"/>

<br></br>
The analysis revealed a 20.37% churn rate, exposing $185.59M in customer balances, while Germany recorded 32.44% churn and customers holding 3–4 products exhibited exceptionally high churn. A Gradient Boosting model achieved a PR-AUC of 0.71, and threshold optimization captured 89.2% of actual churners on the test set. These findings support targeted retention campaigns, customer engagement improvement, product suitability reviews, and financially prioritized churn prevention.
